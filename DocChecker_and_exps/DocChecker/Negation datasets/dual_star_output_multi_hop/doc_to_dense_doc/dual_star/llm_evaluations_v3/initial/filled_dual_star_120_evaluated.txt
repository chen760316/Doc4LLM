Rule: 5. Q[9, 10](field_of_award(Nobel_Prize, Physiology_or_Medicine) ∧ nationality(Barry_Marshall, Australian) → nationality(Robin_Warren, Australian))
Contradiction Explanation: This rule states that if Barry Marshall is of Australian nationality and the Nobel Prize is awarded in the field of Physiology or Medicine, then Robin Warren must also be of Australian nationality. This contradicts the factual nature of nationality, as it cannot be inferred that two individuals share the same nationality based solely on the field of an award.
