S1: Barajas-Hernandez next contends that the district court erred in imposing a 16-level enhancement to his sentence, because his 1988 conviction for aggravated assault was not considered an aggravated felony for purposes of 8 U.S.C. § 1326(b)(2) at the time of its commission.
S2: This contention is foreclosed by our decision in United States v. Maria-Gonzalez, 268 F.3d 664, 669 (9th Cir.2001) (<HOLDING>); United States v. Mendoza-Iribe, 198 F.3d 742, holding that the expanded definition of aggravated felony applies retroactively in actions taken on or after september 30 1996.
