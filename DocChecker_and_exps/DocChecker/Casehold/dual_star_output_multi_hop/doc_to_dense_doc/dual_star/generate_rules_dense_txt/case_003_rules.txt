S1: The “chain of events” language on which Supreme Beef relies is part of the court’s unremarkable holding that one cannot separate the underlying debt (the unpaid taxes) from the enforcement action (the levy), when discussing whether the levy and the counterclaim arose from the same transaction or occurrence.
S2: Further, this Court has implicitly rejected this “chain of events” notion when common facts and legal issues are not present.
