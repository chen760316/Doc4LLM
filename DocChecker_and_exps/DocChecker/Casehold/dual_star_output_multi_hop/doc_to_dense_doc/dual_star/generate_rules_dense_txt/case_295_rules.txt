S1: a criminal defendant’s constitutional right to due process does not entitle him to a prosecution free of such appearances.
S2: Instead, a criminal defendant is denied due process only when his former counsel joins a Commonwealth’s Attorney’s office and is not effectively screened from contact with the Commonwealth’s attorneys who are handling the defendant’s case on a related matter.
