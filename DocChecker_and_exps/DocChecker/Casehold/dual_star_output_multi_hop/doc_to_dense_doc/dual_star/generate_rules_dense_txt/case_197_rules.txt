S1: federal claim is deemed ‘inextricably intertwined’ with the state-court judgment if the federal claim succeeds only to the extent that the state court wrongly decided the issues before it.
S2: Thus, pursuant to Rooker-Feldman, federal courts (other than the Supreme Court) cannot review state court judgments for errors in construing federal law.
