S1: if the law of the country of habitual residence “confers upon the father the joint right to determine his child’s country of residence,” that joint right “is a right of custody under the Convention.”
S2: the Hague Convention only requires the return of a child if the removal was “wrongful,” which occurs if the parent who removed the child did so “in breach of rights of custody” of the other parent.
