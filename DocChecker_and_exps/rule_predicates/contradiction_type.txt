Type
Definition
Original Statement
Generated Self-Contradiction

Type1:
Factual
Need external world knowledge to confirm the contradiction.
The road T51 was located in New York.
The road T51 was located in California.

Type2:
Content/Opinion/Emotion/Misinformation/Semantic/Lexical
Changing one/multiple attributes of an event or entity
Zully Broussard donated her kidney to a stranger.
Zully Broussard donated her kidney to her close friend.

Type3:
Causal
The effect does not match the cause.
I slam the door.
After I do that, the door opens.


Type4:
Negation/Antonym
Negating the original sentence
Zully donated her kidney.
Zully never donated her kidney.

Type5:
Relational/Structure
Description of two mutually exclusive relations between entities.
Jane and Tom are a married couple.
Jane is Tom’s sister.

Type6:
Numeric
Number mismatch or number out of scope.
All the donors are between 20 to 45 years old.
Lisa, who donates her kidney, she is 70 years old.

Type7:
Temporal
时序冲突是指知识随时间推移发生变化或更新时所引发的矛盾。当新知识出现时，旧有知识会逐渐过时或被淘汰，从而导致关于同一实体的信息出现不一致
Around 1860, Nobel and other researches believed that nitroglycerin could not treat heart disease
After Nobel’s death, further research revealed that nitroglycerin is effective for treating heart disease
